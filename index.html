WHY I BUILD

Technology should support wellbeing, not just showcase technology.

My apps began as personal solutions to problems I needed to deal with myself. They explore practical ways technology can support wellbeing — from managing environmental challenges and reducing smoking to personal safety and making limited resources go further. Instead of building technology simply for its own sake, I want to turn useful ideas into tools that can make other people's lives a little easier. Every line of code is forged from my own challenges, turning past hardships into functional tools for resilience. I don’t just build apps; I engineer the solutions I once desperately needed, so that others don't have to navigate their struggles alone.
