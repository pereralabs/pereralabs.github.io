ABOUT ME

From my dad's first IBM 386 to building technology that supports wellbeing.

My journey in technology started at a very early age in hardware—opening up, troubleshooting, and learning the fundamentals on my dad's first IBM 386. From building and repairing machines, I naturally progressed into networking and systems infrastructure, learning how data moves and how connected environments stay resilient. Over time, I discovered that my true niche, creative enjoyment, and deepest satisfaction live in UI/UX design and software development—crafting thoughtful interfaces and writing code that directly helps people—while continuing to explore and study cybersecurity as a hobby.

My apps began as personal solutions to problems I needed to deal with myself. They explore practical ways technology can support wellbeing — from managing environmental light & heat challenges and reducing smoking to personal safety and making limited resources go further. Every line of code is forged from real-world necessity, turning past hardships into functional tools for everyday resilience.
