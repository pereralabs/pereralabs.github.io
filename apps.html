ANDROID · ENVIRONMENTAL TOOLKIT

Sky Cover

An app designed to help people who are highly sensitive to bright light and heat, whether from medication side effects or other circumstances. It brings together practical environmental tools for finding shade and planning around the sun.

  • Find and use cloud cover to plan outdoor time
  • Sun-position tools for practical daily planning
  • UV-related tools for exploring sunlight as a cleaning resource
  • Solar-panel angle guidance
  • Night-time stargazing tools
  • Moon progression information

ANDROID · WEAR OS · PHONE COMPANION

Call Me Now!

A discreet fake-call system designed to help someone exit an uncomfortable situation. The project includes a Wear OS experience for the smartwatch and a companion Android phone app, giving the user a quick way to trigger a believable incoming call.

  • Smartwatch-first interaction
  • Android phone companion
  • Discreet trigger and customizable call experience
  • Designed around real-world personal situations

ANDROID · TRAVEL · BUDGETING

CheapoTrip

A budget travel planner built around one simple idea: seeing the world should be possible without spending recklessly. The app focuses on planning trips, controlling spending and making limited travel budgets go further.

  • Budget-focused trip planning
  • Frugal spending tools
  • Planning around transportation and accommodation costs
  • Designed for travelers who want to see more while spending less

WEAR OS · ANDROID · HEALTH & WELLNESS

Smoke Out

A smartwatch-first wellness app designed to help people reduce their smoking gradually. Set a personal target, keep track of progress and receive encouraging health facts along the way.

  • Set a personal cigarette-reduction target
  • Track smoking and progress toward the target
  • Smartwatch experience for quick, in-the-moment interaction
  • Android phone companion app
  • Encouraging health and wellness facts to reinforce progress
  • Focused on gradual reduction and positive reinforcement
