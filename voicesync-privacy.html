Privacy Policy for VoiceSync Studio

Last updated: October 29, 2026

VoiceSync Studio ("the Application") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the Application.

1. Information Collection and Storage

VoiceSync Studio is a local desktop application. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage metrics, or user data. All audio files, synchronization data, and project assets processed by the Application remain strictly on your local device.

2. Network and Internet Access

The Application functions entirely offline and does not require an active internet connection to operate, except where necessary for system-level installation or updates managed by the Microsoft Store. The Application does not send telemetry, analytics, or background diagnostics to external servers.

3. Third-Party Access

Because no personal data is collected or stored, no information is sold, rented, or disclosed to any third parties.
