Verify Your Downloaded APK in Seconds

Every self-distributed Perera Labs APK is signed with our hardware-backed release keystore and published with its SHA-256 digest so you can verify package integrity on Windows, macOS, or Linux prior to sideloading.

# PowerShell (Windows) — Verify Sky Cover v1.5 APK
Get-FileHash .\skycover-release.apk -Algorithm SHA256
# Expected: 946ebd68e0f46d495ed0835513f119fe52193703cd976467540d4edfb3f7920c
